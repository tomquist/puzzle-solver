# Physical Jigsaw Puzzle Market, Customers, and B2B / Partnership / Affiliate Opportunities for "Puzzle Solver"

Research date: 2026-10-21. About 20 tool calls (searches and fetches). Many market-report numbers are paywalled headline figures and are labelled as such. Where a summary came from a search snippet and not from a fetched primary page, that is noted.

## 1. Market size, growth and post-pandemic trends

### Takeaway
Paywalled analyst headlines put the global jigsaw puzzle market at roughly US$2.2–2.8B in 2025, growing about 3–5% a year. These are unverified estimates, and one outlier says $9.65B. The verified signals are company-reported. Ravensburger's games/puzzles/books core grew 3% in 2025, adult puzzles were a "bright spot", and US/Canada sales rose 11%. Schmidt Spiele's puzzle segment grew 3% in 2024 and 19% in 2025. The pandemic boom (2020) created a lasting adult puzzler base, and puzzles have now settled into steady low-single-digit growth, with competitive/speed puzzling as the new growth story.

### Cited Findings
**Market-size headlines (paywalled, unverified methodology)**
- Fortune Business Insights: global jigsaw market US$2.23B in 2025, forecast US$3.36B by 2034, 4.73% CAGR (2026–2034). North America held 35.35% of the market in 2025. — [Fortune Business Insights via search summary](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/index.php/puzzle-markt-110601)
- SkyQuest: US$2.68B (2024), US$2.76B (2025), 3.1% CAGR to 2033. — [GII / SkyQuest listing](https://www.giiresearch.com/report/sky1917151-jigsaw-puzzle-market-size-share-growth-analysis-by.html)
- Report Prime: US$9.65B (2025) to US$13.43B (2032), 4.84% CAGR. This is an outlier, the scope appears broader, and the listing is miscategorized. Treat with caution. — [Report Prime](https://www.reportprime.com/jigsaw-puzzle-r2915.md)
- Statista Market Insights, US "Puzzles & Knobelspiele" (puzzles plus brain teasers, broader than jigsaws): about €924.5M in 2025, the largest country market. That is about €2.69 per capita, forecast €1.09B by 2029 (4.25% CAGR 2025–2029). — [Statista DE outlook, via search snippet](https://de.statista.com/outlook/cmo/spielzeug-hobby/spielzeug-spiele/puzzles-knobelspiele/usa)
- Statista ranks the US first by revenue in the "Puzzles" segment (2024). Numbers are masked. — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1331920/leading-puzzle-markets-worldwide)

**Verified / company-reported signals**
- Ravensburger 2025: group sales €744M, down 5.9% (down 4.8% currency-adjusted), driven by Disney Lorcana trading cards normalizing. The core games/puzzles/books business grew 3%. Adult puzzles were a strong international seller. US and Canada sales rose 11% and Eastern Europe 12%. Ravensburger is "investing more in live events, tournaments, and speed puzzling competitions." It also mentions new digital printing for personalized/limited-edition products. No puzzle-only revenue is disclosed. — [Spielwarenmesse, fetched](https://www.spielwarenmesse.de/en/mag/toy-market-news/ravensburger-2025-growth-in-core-business-drop-in-trading-cards/)
- Ravensburger 2023 turnover: €669M. — [Businesscoot](https://businesscoot.com/en/company/ravensburger)
- Schmidt Spiele 2024: total sales about €57.5M (roughly flat), puzzle segment up 3%, exports up 11%. — [BoardGameWire](https://boardgamewire.com/index.php/2025/01/22/kennerspiel-des-jahres-winner-schmidt-spiele-scores-11-sales-growth-outside-germany-total-2024-sales-reached-e57-5m/); [Spielwarenmesse](https://www.spielwarenmesse.de/de/magazin/branchennews/annual-balance-sheet-2024-schmidt-spiele-with-stable-sales-at-a-high-level)
- Schmidt Spiele 2025: revenue €61.5M, puzzle segment up 19% (from search summary, not fetched). — [Spielwarenmesse](https://www.spielwarenmesse.de/en/mag/toy-market-news/schmidt-spiele-reports-successful-financial-year/)
- Circana US toy market H1 2025: dollar sales up 6% and units up 3%, after a 2023 decline and a flat 2024. The "Games & Puzzles" supercategory was up 39% in dollars, mainly from Pokémon/NFL trading cards, so this is NOT puzzle-specific. Sales to recipients aged 18+ rose 18% in H1 2025. Adults (kidults) are the fastest-growing segment. — [Circana](https://www.circana.com/post/us-toy-market-is-growing-in-2025-circana-reports); [License Global](https://www.licenseglobal.com/toys-games/circana-report-u-s-toy-market-is-growing-in-2025)

**Pandemic boom (2020, historical)**
- Ravensburger's North American puzzle sales rose 370% year over year over two weeks in March 2020. Sources differ on whether the figure is US or North America. Ravensburger said it averaged 20 puzzles sold per minute in 2020, versus 7 per minute in 2019. — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/puzzle-sales-boom-as-families-unplug-during-coronavirus-lockdown/); [NPR, Apr 2020](https://www.npr.org/sections/coronavirus-live-updates/2020/04/13/833346707/with-people-stuck-at-home-jigsaw-puzzle-sales-soar)
- Ravensburger sold 28M puzzles in 2020, a third more than in 2019. — [Rheinpfalz](https://www.rheinpfalz.de/wirtschaft_artikel,-ravensburger-profitiert-in-pandemie-von-puzzle-boom-_arid,5161420.html) (via search summary; paywalled article)
- Buffalo Games sold more than 1M puzzles from mid-March to April 2020, a weekly demand jump of about 1,500%. — [WKBW](https://www.wkbw.com/news/coronavirus/buffalo-puzzle-manufacturer-seeing-explosion-in-demand)

### Inferences
- A reasonable working figure is a global market of about US$2–3B, with the US the largest single market at about US$0.9–1.0B (including brain teasers) and North America about 35%. Analysts' growth rates converge on about 3–5%.
- Ravensburger alone probably sells tens of millions of puzzles a year (28M in 2020). With 20 puzzles a minute, that is about 10.5M units a year at the boom rate. The installed base of boxes the app could be pointed at is very large.
- Industry growth is now coming from adults and from events/competitions. Both fit an assistant app aimed at adult 500–1000+ piece puzzlers.

### Gaps
- No verified, non-paywalled global puzzle-only market total. The analyst methodologies are not visible.
- No reliable EU-specific jigsaw market size found.
- No reliable count of "active puzzlers" globally.
- No verified average puzzle price or puzzles bought per year per puzzler. The Statista per-capita figure (€2.69 per US resident) is the only spending proxy found.
- No Circana puzzle-only (ex-trading-card) growth figure for 2024–2025.

## 2. Demographics and customer segments

### Takeaway
The best primary survey is old: Ipsos for Ravensburger, January 2019. It found that 48% of US adults enjoy 500+ piece puzzles at least once a year and 19% puzzle at least monthly. Monthly puzzling skews younger, male and toward parents. Purchasers are widely reported as majority female, but that figure is unverified. Seniors, dementia care and families are clear use segments, though without good quantification.

### Cited Findings
- Ipsos/Ravensburger (released Jan 3, 2019, US adults, online survey, sample size not stated on page): 48% of US adults enjoy puzzling (500+ piece adult puzzles) at least once a year. 19% puzzle monthly, weekly or daily. — [Ipsos, fetched](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/node/478706)
- Same survey: 24% of men versus 14% of women puzzle at least monthly. By age, 34% of 18–34s versus 10% of those 55+. Adults with children at home: 28% versus 15% without. — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/node/478706); [Ipsos PDF](https://www.ipsos.com/sites/default/files/ct/news/documents/2019-01/ravensburger-2018-puzzle-survey-pr-01-03-2019.pdf)
- Top reasons: relaxation (59%), fun (57%) and stress relief. These are especially cited by women and older adults. — [Ipsos](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/node/478706) (percentages from search summary of the PDF)
- Unverified aggregator claims: women made up 62% of global puzzle purchasers in 2023, and 45% of US buyers were aged 35–54 (attributed to Statista, not traceable). — [Gitnux](https://gitnux.org/puzzle-industry-statistics/). A "seniors are 20% of daily solvers" claim has no source. — [WifiTalents](https://wifitalents.com/puzzle-industry-statistics/). Do not rely on these.
- Statista has a Q4 2022 US puzzle popularity by gender chart, which is paywalled. — [Statista](https://statista.com/statistics/1327222/puzzles-popularity-gender-usa)
- Dementia/therapy: a Cochrane-cited claim says mental stimulation such as jigsaws improved memory/thinking scores in 718 people with mild-to-moderate dementia (secondary toy-trade source). — [ToyWorld](https://toyworldmag.co.uk/?p=147366). An Alzheimer's Society user panel found that a picture on the box, sturdy pieces and backing boards with guidelines help people with dementia complete puzzles. — [Alzheimer's Society](https://www.alzheimers.org.uk/info/20082/living_with_dementia_magazine/248/trying_out_a_range_of_activity_products_for_people_with_dementia). Specialist vendors sell 13–16 piece dementia jigsaws to care homes. — [Training2Care](https://www.training2care.com/products/activities/les-ives-family-dementia-jigsaw.htm); [Activities for Care (AU)](https://activitiesforcare.com.au/product-tag/jigsaw-puzzles)
- Research prototype "Jigsawdio" (tablet audio-visual reminiscence puzzles for dementia) reported improved cognition and reduced depression. This is a preliminary conference abstract. — [Duke Scholars](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1691014); see also [PMC review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10736734)
- Difficulty benchmark from MySpeedPuzzling: the median solo solve time for 1000 pieces is 3h 16min across 16,709 logged solves. These are enthusiasts, so typical hobbyists are much slower. — [MySpeedPuzzling](https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/for-organizers) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- About 48% of roughly 260M US adults implies on the order of 125M annual US puzzlers, with about 19% (about 50M) at least monthly (2018 data, before the pandemic). These are upper-bound "enjoy puzzling" figures, not buyers.
- The app's best-fit users are frequent 500–1000+ piece puzzlers who get stuck on large same-colour areas, plus families with children (28% puzzle monthly). Older adults puzzle less often but cite relaxation, so they are a softer, assistive-tech segment.
- Low-vision / dementia angle: an app that photographs the box image and points at the piece's location is a plausible accessibility aid. However, the dementia segment mostly uses 13–100 piece puzzles, where the app adds little value. Carers of mid-stage users at 300–500 pieces are a better target.

### Gaps
- No 2023–2026 survey on puzzler demographics, income, region or share doing 1000+ pieces.
- No data found on low-vision puzzlers or on accessibility products for jigsaws.
- No verified spending per puzzler.

## 3. Major manufacturers: revenues, digital initiatives, replacement services, tech partnerships

### Takeaway
Ravensburger (€744M group, 2025) is the clear leader and the most plausible partner. It has its own digital puzzle apps and does tournaments. Its missing-piece service is limited: spare pieces are generally not kept except for 9000+ piece puzzles, and the US offers a 365-day "Care Commitment" replacement of whole products. No manufacturer was found offering a camera-based piece-placement tool or partnering with a tech company on one.

### Cited Findings
- Ravensburger: 2025 group sales €744M, 2023 €669M (see §1). Strategy includes speed-puzzling events and digital printing for personalization. — [Spielwarenmesse](https://www.spielwarenmesse.de/en/mag/toy-market-news/ravensburger-2025-growth-in-core-business-drop-in-trading-cards/)
- Ravensburger digital: "Ravensburger Puzzle" iPad app (2014, €2.99, 20–500 pieces, in-app coin purchase for motifs, later auto-sorting/edge assembly). It has been given away free at times. "Puzzle World" is a browser-based multiplayer digital jigsaw. These are virtual jigsaws, not aids for physical puzzles. — [TouchArcade 2014](https://toucharcade.com/2014/03/19/gdc-2014-ravensburger-puzzle/); [ifun.de](https://www.ifun.de/spiele-tipp-ravensburger-puzzle-app-fuer-ios-86182/); [appgefahren](https://www.appgefahren.de/?p=165912); [Hip2Save](https://hip2save.com/?p=10140442)
- Ravensburger missing pieces (UK, ≤5000 pieces): no spare pieces are kept. If the puzzle was bought within 6 months, Ravensburger may provide a replacement puzzle, voucher or refund. — [Ravensburger support](https://ravensburger-en.mindtouch.us/Service_and_Support/Faulty_Items/Missing_a_puzzle_piece_(5000_pieces_or_less_puzzles))
- Ravensburger 9000+ pieces: matching replacement pieces are possible. Customers must supply a photo, bag letter, control number and the **grid reference** of the missing piece. — [Ravensburger support](https://ravensburger-en.mindtouch.us/Service_and_Support/Faulty_Items/Missing_a_puzzle_piece_(9000__piece_puzzles))
- Ravensburger US "Care Commitment": 365-day replacement for products bought from the Ravensburger store or authorized US sellers. Proof of purchase may be required. — [Ravensburger US](https://www.ravensburger.us/en-US/service/care-commitment)
- Trustpilot reviewers report refusals for puzzles more than 6 months old and that individual pieces are not replaced. — [Trustpilot UK](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/www.ravensburger.co.uk)
- Schmidt Spiele: about €57.5M (2024) and €61.5M (2025), with the puzzle segment growing (see §1).
- Report Prime lists Educa Borras at about US$0.22B and Schmidt at about US$0.25B (2024). The Schmidt figure conflicts with Schmidt's reported €57.5M, so these estimates are unreliable. — [Report Prime](https://www.reportprime.com/jigsaw-puzzle-r2915/company)
- Competitive landscape for camera-based piece finding: only academic prototypes were found. Harvey Mudd "Puzzle Master 9000" (2020, pairwise fit from photo of loose pieces) and WPI MQP "Jigsaw Puzzle Solver" (photos of pieces plus finished image; accuracy drops as piece count rises). — [HMC Clinic](https://hmc.edu/clinic/2020/puzzle-master-9000-the-jigsaw-puzzle-helping-app); [WPI](https://digital.wpi.edu/concern/student_works/qf85nf844?locale=en)

### Inferences
- Ravensburger's 9000+ piece process already asks customers for a "grid reference" of the missing piece. An app that maps pieces onto the box image could produce that reference automatically and could reduce customer-service cost. That gives a concrete B2B pitch: "missing-piece identification" or a customer-service integration.
- Manufacturers mostly do not replace single pieces for ordinary puzzles. Users who lose pieces have no remedy, which limits the "replacement service" tie-in. The value is instead in helping users finish (fewer abandoned puzzles and negative reviews).
- Box QR codes are a plausible distribution channel (scan the box to open the app pre-loaded with the box image). However, I found no evidence that any manufacturer currently prints QR codes linking to solving aids.

### Gaps
- No revenue data found for Buffalo Games (private), Clementoni, Springbok, Cobble Hill (Outset Media), Galison (Mudpuppy/Abrams), Liberty, Pomegranate or Eurographics.
- No evidence found of QR codes on puzzle boxes or of prior app/licensing deals between puzzle makers and tech companies (beyond Ravensburger's own digital jigsaw apps).
- Buffalo Games' and other manufacturers' replacement policies were not researched in detail.

## 4. Subscriptions, rentals, libraries, cafés and competitions

### Takeaway
This is a fragmented, small-business ecosystem: mail-rental subscriptions (about $28/month), premium wooden-puzzle clubs (about $195–200 per 6 months), free or charity community puzzle libraries (UK), and fast-growing competitive speed puzzling (WJPF Worlds with 72 countries in 2025; USAJPA; MySpeedPuzzling). These are better seen as distribution/co-marketing partners than as paying B2B customers. Speed puzzlers would likely consider assistance tools contrary to competition, though they are useful for training and tracking.

### Cited Findings
- "Completing the Puzzle" mail rental: $28/month on a 6-month plan, with a prepaid return label. — [WFLA](https://www.wfla.com/bloom-tampa-bay/bloom-lifestyle/subscription-service-lets-you-rent-puzzles-by-mail/amp/)
- Hoefnagel/Artifact wooden "Puzzle Club" (private lending library with a wishlist): about $195–200 per 6 months. — [Artifact Puzzles](https://www.artifactpuzzles.com/products/6-month-puzzle-club-subscription)
- UK community jigsaw libraries: Stourbridge Jigsaw Library charges £2 per puzzle, with proceeds going to a hospice. Library and county services lend puzzles on an honesty system. — [Mary Stevens Hospice](https://www.marystevenshospice.co.uk/stourbridge-jigsaw-library); [Warwickshire](https://searchout.warwickshire.gov.uk/group/323/jigsaw-puzzle-library); [Culture PK](https://culturepk.org.uk/libraries/services-and-programs/jigsawexchange/)
- WJPF World Jigsaw Puzzle Championship 2025 (Valladolid, Spain): 72 countries represented, with organizers citing participation records. — [CBS News SF](https://www.cbsnews.com/sanfrancisco/news/world-jigsaw-puzzle-championship-2025-uc-berkeley-student-speed-puzzling)
- USAJPA: a non-profit that runs a national competitive framework and the JPAR ranking. Membership is required for times to count toward JPAR but not to compete. Its president is Valerie Coit. — [WDIO](https://wdio.com/archive/northland-speed-puzzlers-headed-to-world-championship); [Chicago Puzzle Club event](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/chicagopuzzleclub/1881406)
- Local public libraries run speed-puzzling events (e.g., Mid-Columbia Libraries). — [Mid-Columbia Libraries](https://midcolumbialibraries.org/speed-puzzling)
- MySpeedPuzzling: open-source platform where "thousands of puzzlers" track times, collections and stats. It has a puzzle database of thousands for event organizers. — [MySpeedPuzzling](https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/for-organizers); [FAQ](https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/faq)
- Competitive puzzling's rapid rise is covered in mainstream press. — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/articles/piece-piece-inside-rapid-rise-140501949.html)

### Inferences
- Rental services and libraries have a real pain point: returned puzzles with missing pieces, and checking completeness before re-lending. A "piece count/verify which pieces are missing" feature could be a paid B2B tool for them. However, these are small businesses and charities with low budgets.
- MySpeedPuzzling (open source, puzzle database) is a natural integration partner for puzzle metadata or box images. That is a partnership rather than a revenue source.
- Puzzle cafés exist anecdotally, but none were documented in this research.

### Gaps
- No count of US/EU puzzle rental subscribers, puzzle cafés or USAJPA members.
- No evidence that any of these organizations buy software tools.
- No WJPF total competitor headcount.

## 5. Affiliate economics and puzzle databases/trackers

### Takeaway
Amazon Associates US pays 3% on Toys (24-hour cookie). Direct puzzle-maker and puzzle-retailer programs pay about 4–8% with 30–90 day cookies. On a typical $15–25 puzzle, that is about $0.45–$2.00 per referred sale, so affiliate revenue only works at large scale or with a "buy the next puzzle" or "find a puzzle you lost pieces to" flow.

### Cited Findings
- Amazon Associates US: Toys at 3%, in the same tier as Furniture, Home, Pets and Sports. The 24-hour cookie extends to 90 days if the item is added to the cart. The guide is dated Oct 14, 2026. The official fee schedule is login-gated and was not fetched directly. — [Lasso, fetched](https://getlasso.co/amazon-affiliate-commission-rate/). It is corroborated by [WPManageNinja (3% Toys & Games)](https://wpmanageninja.com/21-approaches-to-make-money-with-amazon-affiliate-program/). An older 5% figure dates from January 2023 or earlier and appears superseded.
- Ravensburger affiliate: Sale Gains/revshare lists 4%, first purchase, 30-day cookie (updated Aug 31, 2026). — [revshare.so](https://www.revshare.so/programs/advertiser/ravensburger-8c28a9ba). FlexOffers Ravensburger DE lists 4% (3.2% for UK/DE/FR/US), 30-day cookie. — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/ravensburger-de-affiliate-program/). Another FlexOffers listing shows 7.2% and a 59-day cookie, but its status is unclear or inactive. — [FlexOffers](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/ravensburger-affiliate-program). Awin programs (US/DE/FR) are listed as closed. Impact is listed as open. — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/ravensburger)
- Buffalo Games: the Impact.com program is listed as open, and FlexOffers/ShareASale/TradeDoubler listings are closed. No rate was found. — [affi.io](https://affi.io/m/buffalogames-com)
- Puzzle retailers: The Happy Puzzle Company (Awin) up to 7%, 30-day cookie. Go Puzzle FR (Daisycon) 7%. Puzzle.at (Daisycon) 8%, 90-day cookie. All Jigsaw Puzzles (FlexOffers) 4%. JigsawPuzzle.co.uk (FlexOffers) 6%. — [Awin](https://ui.awin.com/merchant-profile/72473); [Daisycon Go Puzzle](https://daisycon.com/es/campanas/20851-go-puzzle-fr); [affi.io puzzle-at](https://affi.io/m/puzzle-at); [FlexOffers All Jigsaw](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/all-jigsaw-puzzles-affiliate-program/); [FlexOffers JigsawPuzzle.co.uk](https://www.flexoffers.com/affiliate-programs/jigsawpuzzle-co-uk-affiliate-program/)
- Puzzle Offensive (DE retailer carrying Cobble Hill and others) runs its own Daisycon partner program. — [Daisycon](https://daisycon.com/sv/kampanj/20852-puzzle-offensive-de)
- Puzzle databases/trackers: MySpeedPuzzling (open source, collections and times). — [MySpeedPuzzling](https://myspeedpuzzling.com/en/faq)

### Inferences
- Example: 10,000 monthly active users × 1 affiliate purchase per user per year × $20 × 4% ≈ $8,000 a year. Affiliate revenue is a supplement, not a business model.
- The best affiliate hook: after the app identifies the puzzle from the box photo, show a "buy this or similar puzzle" or "next puzzle from this brand" link. Higher-rate specialist retailers (6–8%) beat Amazon's 3%, but Amazon converts better.
- Privacy-first on-device architecture is compatible with affiliate links, because these are outbound links with no tracking inside the app. Disclosure (FTC) is required.

### Gaps
- No affiliate program found for Puzzle Warehouse, Cobble Hill, Springbok, Galison, Pomegranate or Eurographics. Buffalo Games' rate is unknown.
- I could not fetch Amazon's official fee schedule because it requires a login.
- Puzzle tracker apps such as "Puzzle Pal"/"MyPuzzles" were not researched. No user counts were found.

## 6. Senior-care / memory-care / OT institutional buyers

### Takeaway
Care homes clearly buy physical dementia jigsaws (13–16 pieces, wipeable, reminiscence imagery) from specialist activity suppliers. I found no evidence that they buy jigsaw-assistance apps. Digital dementia jigsaws exist only as research prototypes (Jigsawdio).

### Cited Findings
- Specialist suppliers (Training2Care UK, Activities for Care AU, Quokka via Kohl's) sell low-piece dementia jigsaws to care settings. — [Training2Care](https://www.training2care.com/products/activities/elvis-dementia-jigsaw.htm); [Activities for Care](https://activitiesforcare.com.au/product-tag/jigsaw-puzzles); [Kohl's/Quokka](https://www.kohls.com/product/prd-8076001/quokka-set-of-3-dementia-jigsaw-puzzles-with-birds-cats-13-pc-alzheimers-puzzle-for-elderly.jsp)
- Gibsons planned a 4–48 piece care line (via search summary). — [ToyWorld](https://toyworldmag.co.uk/?p=147366)
- Jigsawdio tablet prototype (see §2). — [Duke Scholars](https://scholars.duke.edu/publication/1691014)

### Inferences
- The institutional route is weak for this specific app. Dementia puzzles have too few pieces to need the app, and care facilities buy through activity-supply catalogues, not app stores. A possible niche is carer-assisted puzzling at 300–500 pieces, or partnering with an activity supplier to bundle the app. This is unproven.
- The accessibility (low vision) positioning is better used as marketing and grant/award material than as a B2B revenue line.

### Gaps
- No evidence of memory-care or OT purchasing of puzzle apps, nor of budgets for activity software.
- No data on low-vision puzzlers.

## 7. Community size

### Takeaway
The online community is mid-sized and growing. r/Jigsawpuzzles has about 126K members (+23% a year) and the top YouTube creator (Karen Puzzles) has about 289K subscribers. These are third-party tracker figures, and the snapshot dates are unclear. Facebook group and TikTok figures were not found.

### Cited Findings
- r/Jigsawpuzzles: about 126K members, +24K (23.1%) yearly growth (GummySearch snapshot, date unclear). — [GummySearch](https://gummysearch.com/r/Jigsawpuzzles/)
- r/jigsawpuzzlers: about 500 members (small). — [Reddit mirror](https://r.datuan.dev/r/jigsawpuzzlers)
- Karen Puzzles YouTube: about 289K subscribers and about 285 videos (PlayBoard snapshot). An earlier news feature said 200K+ followers and described her as having turned puzzling into a career. — [PlayBoard](https://playboard.co/en/search?q=jigsaw); [Scripps News](https://www.scrippsnews.com/life/holidays-and-celebrations/meet-the-woman-who-turned-finishing-puzzles-into-her-career)
- Puzzle Talk Substack covers WJPF deep dives (a niche media channel). — [Puzzle Talk](https://puzzletalk.substack.com/p/deep-dive-to-world-jigsaw-puzzle/comments)

### Inferences
- Creator sponsorships (Karen Puzzles and similar) and Reddit are the most efficient launch channels. A demo video of "photograph a piece, see where it goes" is inherently shareable content.
- Some community members see assistance as "cheating". Positioning the app as "for when you're stuck / for sorting / for accessibility" will matter.

### Gaps
- No Facebook puzzle group or puzzle-exchange group sizes found.
- No TikTok creator follower counts found.
- Subscriber counts are from third-party trackers, not first-party.
